Purpose of the screening

The Learning Disability Detector & Classifier System (LDCS) is an early screening tool. It looks for possible indicators of learning difficulties, such as dyslexia, dysgraphia and dyscalculia, so that students who may benefit from support can be referred to the Disability Unit.

What information is collected

If you continue, you may be asked to provide handwriting samples, speech, responses to cognitive tasks, and information about your learning and behaviour. This information is personal and may be sensitive.

How your information is used

Your information is analysed by machine learning models to produce a screening flag and a confidence score. It is used only for screening and referral support.

Who can see your results

Your results are visible to you. They are only shared with the Disability Unit if you choose to be referred, and you will be asked separately before that happens.

This is not a diagnosis

The screening result is not a diagnosis and does not replace an assessment by a qualified professional.

Taking part is voluntary

You do not have to take part. You can withdraw your consent at any time from your dashboard. Withdrawing will not affect your access to support services.
