Dear Student,

The Disability Rights Unit (DRU) invites all Students with Disabilities (SWD) to participate in a survey aimed at understanding your experiences as a student with a disability within the university environment. The survey seeks to gather your views on areas such as:
Accessibility of university learning environments
Access to teaching and learning
Reasonable accommodation and support services
Social inclusion and participation in university activities
Recommendations for improving disability inclusion and accessibility at DUT

Your participation has provided valuable insight that can assist the University in strengthening its disability support services and promoting a more inclusive, accessible, and supportive university environment. We encourage you to answer the questions openly and honestly. Your feedback is highly valued; confidentiality will be maintained, and the information collected will be used to better understand the experiences of Students with Disabilities and identify areas requiring improvement.
