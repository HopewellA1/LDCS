Students with Disabilities Survey 2026

Dear Student,

The Disability Rights Unit (DRU) invites all Students with Disabilities (SWD)
to participate in a survey aimed at understanding your experiences as a student
with a disability within the university environment. The survey seeks to gather
your views on areas such as:

  - Accessibility of university learning environments
  - Access to teaching and learning
  - Reasonable accommodation and support services
  - Social inclusion and participation in university activities
  - Recommendations for improving disability inclusion and accessibility at DUT

Your feedback is highly valued; confidentiality will be maintained, and the
information collected will be used to better understand the experiences of
Students with Disabilities and identify areas requiring improvement.
