What can the system screen for?

The proposed system considers multiple learning difficulties rather than screening for a single condition in isolation.

01

Dyslexia

Screening indicators related to reading accuracy, word recognition, spelling and phonological processing.

02

Dysgraphia

Screening indicators associated with writing and handwriting-related difficulties.

03

Dyscalculia

Screening indicators related to number concepts, arithmetic and mathematical relationships.

How the system works

The system provides an early screening layer that supports referral to qualified professionals.

1

Consent

The student provides informed consent before data is collected.

2

Screen

Handwriting, speech, cognitive and behavioural information may be collected.

3

Analyse

Machine learning models analyse patterns within the collected information.

4

Risk Score

The system produces a screening flag and confidence score.

5

Refer

Students identified as at-risk can be referred to the Disability Unit.

Screening is not diagnosis

This system is designed to support early identification and referral. Screening results do not replace assessment or diagnosis by qualified professionals.
